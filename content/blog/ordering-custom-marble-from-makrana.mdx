Ordering a custom marble temple or fountain from Makrana is easier when the design, material and delivery scope are agreed before production. A clear brief gives you a useful quotation and gives the maker a shared reference for approvals.

Legacy Marble is based in Makrana, Rajasthan. If you are comparing a custom marble temple manufacturer or marble fountain manufacturer, use these steps to organise the conversation and assess what each quotation covers.

## Send a brief that describes the project

Begin with the product, location and intended use. A home pooja mandir, a garden fountain and a decorative hotel feature need different drawings and site details.

Include measurements, photographs of the space and reference designs. Explain which features matter most: a particular arch, carving style, tier arrangement, storage layout or overall proportion. A reference photograph works best when paired with notes rather than treated as a complete specification.

For planning help, see the [marble home temple guide](/blog/marble-home-temple-design-guide) or [marble water fountain guide](/blog/marble-water-fountain-buying-guide).

## Ask for a drawing and sample together

Review a dimensioned drawing showing the complete size, important internal spaces and visible joints. Ask the maker to identify parts that will be delivered separately and assembled at the site.

At the same time, approve the proposed material and finish. Keep the sample reference with the drawing so that a design approval is not mistaken for approval of an unspecified stone. Our [marble selection checklist](/blog/white-marble-for-temples-and-fountains) covers the questions to ask about material descriptions and treatments.

## Understand the production method

Ask which stages use machine shaping or CNC work and which involve hand carving and finishing. Focus on what you need to approve: pattern, depth, symmetry, edge treatment and the appearance of the completed surface.

For inlay work, request a separate colour and pattern approval. For detailed carving, agree how photographs or videos will be used for progress reviews. Confirm which changes can still be made at each stage and how a requested change would affect the quotation or schedule.

## Agree the supply scope in writing

A quotation should identify the stonework and any additional components. For a temple, this may involve storage, doors, hardware or a back panel. For a fountain, it may involve the basin, pump, plumbing, lighting or controls.

Ask the supplier to state exclusions as clearly as inclusions. Record taxes, payment stages, estimated production time and the process for approving dispatch. The [temple price guide](/blog/marble-temple-price-in-india) and [fountain price guide](/blog/marble-fountain-price-in-india) provide more detailed comparison checklists.

## Confirm destination and handling before dispatch

Give the destination postcode and describe the route to the final position. If your project is in Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Jaipur or another city, confirm service availability for the actual address rather than assuming a city name guarantees installation coverage.

Discuss packing, transport, insurance, unloading and any lifting equipment. Ask how the consignment should be inspected and how damage should be recorded and reported. Keep the packing and delivery instructions available to the person receiving it.

## Coordinate the installation team

Decide who prepares the support or foundation, assembles the stonework and completes plumbing or electrical work. Share the approved drawings with the responsible site professionals before the product arrives.

For a fountain, arrange the testing and equipment handover. For a mandir, check the agreed alignment, access, storage operation and finish. Keep care instructions and contact details with the final specification.
